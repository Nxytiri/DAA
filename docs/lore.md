# Lore reference

The [Blackridge Complex master archive](https://blackridge-archive.com/) is the public
lore source for Department of Anomalous Affairs. Blackridge Complex is a DAA facility;
the archive includes its chronology, departments, anomalous subjects, and personnel resources.

Useful starting points: [history](https://blackridge-archive.com/history),
[departments](https://blackridge-archive.com/departments),
[anomaly registry](https://blackridge-archive.com/anomalies), and
[facility](https://blackridge-archive.com/facility).

These references provide setting context, not authorization to implement mechanics,
copy archive content wholesale, or connect personnel-account systems.
When a feature depends on a lore detail, cite the specific archive page in that system's
document and confirm the intended gameplay with the owner.
